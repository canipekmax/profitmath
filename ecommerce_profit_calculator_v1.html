What do these terms mean?
COGS

Cost of goods sold — what the product itself costs you.

CAC

Customer acquisition cost — how much you spend to get one customer.

ROAS

Return on ad spend — revenue generated for each unit of advertising spend. This calculator uses realized revenue after the estimated return rate.

Profit margin

Estimated profit divided by realized revenue.

Questions, answered.

Does changing currency convert my numbers?

No. The currency selector changes the denomination and formatting only. If you switch from CAD to USD, a value of 50 remains 50; it is not converted using an exchange rate.

Is this the same as accounting profit?

No. This is a planning calculator designed to estimate unit economics. Taxes, overhead, salaries, storage, returns handling and other business expenses may need to be added separately.

How is the return rate handled?

The current version reduces realized revenue by the estimated return/refund percentage. It does not separately model return shipping, restocking costs or recovered inventory.

What is break-even CAC?

It is the maximum customer acquisition cost that leaves the sale at approximately zero profit under the assumptions you entered.
